## Tinkerers’ Lab?

This is a space for students who are curious about technology, enjoy exploring
new ideas and love creating things. It brings together students from different
branches and backgrounds who share an interest in learning beyond the classroom
and gaining hands-on experience.

From robotics and electronics to 3D printing and project development, there's
always something happening at the lab. Through workshops, technical sessions,
competitions and collaborative projects, students get the chance to experiment,
build and learn from one another. Whether it's figuring out a new concept,
working through a project or simply exchanging ideas, there's always something
to learn from each other.

## What’s the story?

To speak of the story, we have to begin with our dearest Dr. Reena Nair, who
was the foremost proponent of such an endeavour in our college. She acted as
the link between us and MakerBhavan Foundation. She took the initiative
to manage the paperworks and formalities involved in bringing this lab.

![](/images/about-us/nair-with-natekar.jpg)

MakerBhavan Foundation is a non-profit Section 8 company registered in Mumbai,
Maharashtra, and also registered in the U.S. as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public
charity organization, in Palo Alto, California. It acts as the legal parent
corporate of everything Tinkerers’ Lab.

In 2014, the IIT Bombay Class of 1975 envisioned a dedicated 24×7 makerspace
to inspire creativity. In collaborations and partnerships with top educational
institutions, with a shared vision, they brought Invention Factory® to India
at IIT Gandhinagar in the summer of 2018. And in 2019, MakerBhavan Foundation
was created to operationalize and scale the initiative, to act as a
transnational hub fostering innovation and nurturing emerging talent. In 2022,
they also introduced the Vishwakarma Awards to recognize creative engineering
talents and technical excellence.

Today, MakerBhavan Foundation has 23 Tinkerers’ Lab across India. And one of
them is at GEC Idukki. The management gave away the old drawing hall to make
way for establishing the Lab. Every tool and machinery in the Lab was purchased
and shipped by MBF. It was Dr.&nbsp;Reena Nair, Prof.&nbsp;Ratheesh T. K.,
together with the vanguard student volunteers through night and day that set up
the Lab by the winter of ‘25 and summer of ‘26, and the Lab was officially
inaugurated on the 10th of March.

![](/images/about-us/inauguration-group-photo.jpg)

The Lab has gone through a lot of changes since. Although the core members
remained, there were many revisions of the execom. We’ve worked the furniture,
arrangement, and overall atmosphere inside the Lab. “We” here are the students,
because that’s another fact about the Lab. It is entirely run and managed by
the students, from the repair of machines to accounting of finances. It’s run
by the students for the students, no faculties involved.

## Our finances

We raise funds through events and activities that we organise, and through
sponsorships. These funds are used to maintain the lab, restocking consumables,
repairing and fixing machines, and the overall working of the lab. We’ve a
separate and independent financial committee solely for that purpose.
