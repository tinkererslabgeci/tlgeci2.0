Tinkerers Lab at Government Engineering College, Idukki (GECI), is a space for students who are curious about technology, enjoy exploring new ideas and love creating things. It brings together students from different branches and backgrounds who share an interest in learning beyond the classroom and gaining hands-on experience.

A typical gathering at the lab is all about sharing ideas, learning something new and working together. From robotics and electronics to 3D printing and project development, there's always something happening. Through workshops, technical sessions, competitions and collaborative projects, students get the chance to experiment, build and learn from one another. Whether it's figuring out a new concept, working through a project or simply exchanging ideas, there's always something to learn from each other.

You don't need to be an expert or have prior experience to be part of the community. Whether you're someone who loves building things or just beginning to explore technology, you're always welcome at Tinkerers Lab. Bring your curiosity, share your ideas and be a part of the journey!

## Funding and Support

Tinkerers Lab receives financial support from external sources to carry out its activities and support student projects. It also raises funds through events and activities organised by the team. These funds are used to manage the lab's activities, organise future events and provide students with opportunities to explore new ideas and work on innovative projects.
