## Tinkerers’ Lab?

This is a space for students who are curious about technology, enjoy exploring new ideas
and love creating things. It brings together students from different branches and
backgrounds who share an interest in learning beyond the classroom and gaining hands-on
experience.

From robotics and electronics to 3D printing and project development, there's always
something happening at the lab. Through workshops, technical sessions, competitions and
collaborative projects, students get the chance to experiment, build and learn from one
another. Whether it's figuring out a new concept, working through a project or simply
exchanging ideas, there's always something to learn from each other.

## Our finances

We raise funds through events and activities that we organise, and through sponsorships.
These funds are used to maintain the lab, restocking consumables, repairing and fixing
machines, and the overall working of the lab. We’ve a separate and independent financial
committee solely for that purpose.
