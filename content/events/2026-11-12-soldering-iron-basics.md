Ever wanted to build your own circuit from scratch? This is your chance.

Soldering Iron Basics - LIDAR Circuit making on dotted PCB, presented by SAE
GECI in association with Tinkerers' Lab GECI.

No prior experience needed. We will teach you from zero - how to hold a
soldering iron, how to make clean joints, how to read a circuit, and by the end
of the session YOU will have built a working LIDAR circuit on a dotted PCB with
your own hands.

his is not a theory class. This is 3 hours of pure hands-on building.
