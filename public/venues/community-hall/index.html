Before you arrive
Buzz 204 at the side door; the elevator to level 2 is past the mailboxes.

Getting there & access

Wheelchair accessible

Transit: Two blocks from Central station; bus 12 stops outside. Parking: Free street parking after 6pm; a paid lot is next door.
Gender-neutral washrooms on level 2. A quiet room is available on request. Fragrance-free space; please skip strong scents. Kids welcome, there’s a play corner at the back.
